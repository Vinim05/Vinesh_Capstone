### Project Sought

Our Senior Design Project will focus on developing an automated quantitative trading engine that uses Polymarket prediction data to identify shifts in public sentiment and determine how those shifts could affect publicly traded companies. The system will monitor selected Polymarket topics and track changes in market probabilities over time to detect significant changes in public sentiment. When a shift is detected, an AI-based analysis system will evaluate the topic, determine what the change in sentiment represents, and identify companies or industries that could potentially benefit from the shift. The system will then analyze relevant market data and generate trading signals that can be tested through paper trading using sandbox equity APIs. A centralized dashboard will display Polymarket probability trends, detected sentiment shifts, AI-generated company analysis, trading signals, and portfolio performance. The goal is to determine whether changes in prediction-market sentiment can be used as an early signal for identifying potential opportunities in the stock market.
